Why it's different

Safety is enforced in code, not in the prompt

Every action runs the same gauntlet. If any gate fails, no transaction is built and none is offered to you, so the mistake never reaches your wallet or the mempool.

SCOUT

Scan live APY, TVL and your positions across every venue.

→

RESERVE

Verify sBTC peg health via BIP-341 P2TR derivation.

→

GUARDIAN

Check market safety gates: range, volatility, timing.

→

YTG GATE

7-day projected yield must exceed gas cost times three.

→

POST-CONDITIONS

Bind what the transaction may move. The chain rejects the rest.

→

YOU SIGN

An unsigned transaction goes to your wallet. Nothing moves until you approve.

The gauntlet is not decoration. Ask it to deploy $200 and the YTG gate refuses: at that size a week of yield is worth less than the gas to capture it, so no transaction is offered at all. See it in the preview →

This is also how SmartX is paid. Asking is free. Scans, position reads and any action a gate blocks cost nothing. A fee applies only when you sign a transaction SmartX built, settled over x402. When the gauntlet says an action is not worth doing, SmartX earns nothing from telling you so, which keeps the incentive and the safety pointing the same way. Other agents can also call these skills directly through a metered x402 endpoint.
